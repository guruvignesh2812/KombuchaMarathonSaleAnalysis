# DIVS Kombucha Sales Analysis – Fresh Work Marathon Expo Chennai

## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard presents a complete sales and customer behavior analysis for **DIVS Kombucha** during the **Fresh Work Marathon Expo conducted in Chennai**.

# 🎯 Business Problem

DIVS Kombucha participated in a large-scale marathon expo event to promote and sell kombucha products.

The business wanted answers to:
- Which kombucha flavor performed best?
- Which customer segment purchased the most?
- What age group showed higher engagement?
- Which locations generated stronger sales?
- How satisfied were customers with products?

This dashboard helps stakeholders quickly identify customer trends and optimize future sales campaigns.

# 📈 Insights Derived

## 🥤 Product Performance Analysis
The analysis revealed that:
- **DIVS Alphonso Mango Kombucha** and **DIVS Coconut Kefir** were the top-performing products with the highest purchase count.
- **Strawberry Kombucha** showed comparatively lower sales, indicating possible opportunities for flavor optimization or marketing improvements.

### Business Insight
Fruit-based and tropical flavors attracted more customers during the expo environment.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Gender-Based Purchase Distribution
The purchase distribution between male and female customers was nearly balanced:
- Male Customers: 52.12%
- Female Customers: 47.88%

### Business Insight
The product appeals to both genders equally, making DIVS Kombucha a broadly marketable wellness beverage.

---

## 👥 Demographic Segmentation Analysis
Customer segmentation based on age groups showed:
- Adults contributed the highest number of purchases.
- Senior customers provided the highest average product ratings.
- Youth category had lower purchasing participation.

### Business Insight
Middle-aged and senior health-conscious consumers are currently the strongest target audience for kombucha products.

---

## 🧑‍💼 Customer Occupation Analysis
Top purchasing professions included:
- Doctors
- Fitness Trainers
- Content Creators

### Business Insight
Health-aware professionals demonstrated higher engagement and product adoption during the expo.

This indicates strong alignment between kombucha products and wellness-focused lifestyles.

---

## 📍 Geographic Analysis
The geographic segmentation showed stronger sales concentration in:
- Chennai
- Perambur
- Tiruvottiyur

### Business Insight
Urban and fitness-active regions generated stronger product traction, suggesting ideal target zones for future campaigns and pop-up events.
